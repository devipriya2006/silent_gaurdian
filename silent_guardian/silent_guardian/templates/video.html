How detection works

  1. Motion extraction — dense optical flow is computed between consecutive frames.
  2. Feature scoring — motion energy, spikiness, and directional chaos are combined into a per-frame score, adaptively normalized to the scene's own baseline activity.
  3. Temporal smoothing + dedup — scores are averaged over a short sliding window, and a continuous high-score stretch is treated as a single event rather than raising a new alert every frame.
  4. Alert & evidence capture — HIGH/CRITICAL results save an evidence frame and log an incident for review; CRITICAL results also trigger an external alert if configured.
