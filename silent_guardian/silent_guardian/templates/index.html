Scoring & classification

Every mode reports the same normalized 0–100 risk/confidence indicator, classified consistently:

LOW · 0–29 MODERATE · 30–49 HIGH · 50–74 CRITICAL · 75–100

This score is a model confidence/risk indicator only — it is not proof that violence, abuse, or harassment occurred. HIGH and CRITICAL results are saved for human review rather than acted on automatically.
