How detection works

  1. Motion extraction — dense optical flow is computed between consecutive frames.
  2. Feature scoring — motion energy, spikiness, and directional chaos are combined into a per-frame score, adaptively normalized to the scene's own baseline activity.
  3. Temporal smoothing — scores are averaged over a short sliding window so a single noisy frame can't trigger a false alert.
  4. Alert & evidence capture — once the smoothed score crosses the threshold, an evidence frame is saved, an incident is logged, and an alert (email/webhook) is dispatched, respecting a cooldown to avoid spam.
